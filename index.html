Eduardo Ambriz Lazo

Mechatronics · Mechanical Design · Embedded systems · Biomimetics

Welcome to my engineering portfolio! I’m a senior at Harvey Mudd College studying engineering with a mechanical focus, and I love projects where mechanical design, electronics, and code all have to work together.

Right now I’m a mechatronics clinic engineer on a project sponsored by Blue Origin, and I research flexible-fin motion and fluid dynamics at the Harvey Mudd FILM Lab. Last spring I was a mechanical/R&D engineer on an AirMyne project, studying interfacial structure design to improve how quickly CO2 can be absorbed from the atmosphere.

Outside of class I’m usually working on mechanical and microcontroller projects, making art, playing video games, or rock climbing. Stay curious!
